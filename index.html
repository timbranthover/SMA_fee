RESEARCH & GOVERNANCE

How this prototype is governed

Flags are centrally defined, owned and effective-dated.

Every result is evaluated against the same 130,428-record mock catalog. Counts, filters and sorting are calculated server-side; only the current 25-row page is sent to the browser.

Illustrative data only. Real production use requires authenticated entitlements, approved source systems, audit logging and compliance review.
